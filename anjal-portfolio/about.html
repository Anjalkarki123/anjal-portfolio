About me

Who I am

I'm a Senior Year Computer Science student at the University of Idaho, where I hold a 3.80 GPA. My coursework spans embedded systems, computer organization and architecture, operating systems, data structures and algorithms, software engineering, and machine learning, so I'm comfortable working close to the hardware as well as at the application level.

What connects my work is verification. I like building things, but I like knowing they are correct even more. At my internship I designed a step where a person checks every AI-generated invoice against the source data before it goes out, because extraction output can look perfect while being wrong.

Beyond classes, I've served as Safety & Risk Management Officer for the International Student Organization, as a Peer Mentor, and as a member of the Association for Computing Machinery since 2023.
